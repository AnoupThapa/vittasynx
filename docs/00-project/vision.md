# Product vision

## Product definition
Vittasynx is a financial operations intelligence platform for inventory-led B2B wholesalers and distributors. It helps owners and finance teams understand receivables, customer payment behaviour, upcoming supplier obligations and near-term liquidity.

## Problem
A distributor may have accounting records and spreadsheets but still struggle to answer:
- Which customers are likely to pay late?
- Which overdue invoices need attention first?
- How much cash is reasonably expected over the next 13 weeks?
- Which supplier and operating obligations are approaching?
- What happens if a major customer pays late or an expected order is delayed?

## Core proposition
Know what you are owed, when cash may arrive, what you need to pay and what requires attention.

## Initial customer
Small and medium B2B wholesalers and distributors, initially businesses with approximately 5–50 employees, recurring customers, credit sales and regular supplier obligations.

Potential verticals for discovery include medical and healthcare supplies, cleaning and hygiene products, industrial supplies, packaging, foodservice and business consumables. These are hypotheses, not validated market choices.

## Product principles
1. Accuracy before automation.
2. Evidence before assumptions.
3. Explainable calculations.
4. Privacy and tenant isolation by design.
5. Human review when source data or matching is uncertain.
6. Useful operational decisions rather than decorative dashboards.
7. Start narrow and prove repeated value before expanding.

## Long-term direction
Become a trusted financial operations layer for distributors, connecting customer payment behaviour, receivables, supplier obligations and cash planning. Any industry benchmarking must be permissioned, aggregated, privacy-preserving and supported by sufficient data quality and scale.

## Success measures
- Users return weekly because the product supports real operating decisions.
- Imported data can be reconciled and corrected without losing source history.
- Forecast performance is measured against actual outcomes.
- Customers pay for ongoing use.
- Onboarding and support costs remain commercially sustainable.
