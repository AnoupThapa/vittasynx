# Known limitations

## Before pilot
- Product concept and willingness to pay are not yet validated.
- No production architecture or hosting provider has been selected.
- No accounting integrations are confirmed.
- Forecast accuracy has not been measured.
- Payment-behaviour indicators have not been validated against future outcomes.
- Import templates and supported source formats remain to be finalised.
- Legal and privacy requirements require jurisdiction-specific review.

## Product limitations to disclose
- Forecasts depend on completeness and quality of customer-provided data.
- Expected collection dates are estimates, not promises.
- Payment matching may require manual review.
- A dashboard is not a substitute for accounting, tax, legal or financial advice.
- No creditworthiness conclusion should be inferred from limited payment history.

Update this file when limitations are discovered, resolved or materially changed.
