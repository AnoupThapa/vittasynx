# Data privacy requirements

## Principles
- Data minimisation
- Purpose limitation
- Tenant isolation
- Transparent processing
- Access control
- Retention limitation
- Secure deletion
- Traceable exports and administrative access

## Customer data
Document each data category, purpose, lawful basis where applicable, access roles, retention period, subprocessors and deletion process. Obtain legal review for target markets, including Nepal and Australia, before commercial launch.

## AI and external processors
Do not send customer financial data to an AI provider unless the provider, contract, data handling, retention, training use, region and security terms have been reviewed and approved. Minimise or redact data where possible.

## User rights and customer requests
Define procedures for access, correction, export and deletion requests. Legal retention obligations may affect deletion. Do not promise immediate deletion without checking backups, audit requirements and applicable law.

## Cross-tenant data
Do not expose one customer's raw records, invoices, payments or customer-level behaviour to another customer. Any future benchmark must be aggregated, permissioned and tested against re-identification risk.
