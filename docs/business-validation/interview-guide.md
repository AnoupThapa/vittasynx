# Discovery interview guide

**Status:** Ready for use. No interviews have been conducted yet.

## Purpose
Understand how B2B wholesalers and distributors actually manage receivables, without selling Vittasynx. We are testing assumptions A-01 to A-11 (`docs/09-decisions/assumptions.md`), not seeking agreement.

## Who to interview
Business owners, finance managers and accounts receivable staff at inventory-led B2B businesses selling on credit (roughly 5–50 staff). Candidate segments (hypotheses only): medical and healthcare distributors, cleaning and hygiene wholesalers, industrial supplies, packaging, foodservice. Aim for 20 interviews across at least 3 segments, with no more than 30% from any single business network.

## Ground rules for the interviewer
- Ask about **past behaviour**, not hypothetical future behaviour ("Tell me about the last time…" not "Would you…").
- Don't describe the product until the final section, if at all.
- Don't correct or lead. Silence is fine.
- Never ask for bank passwords, logins or account numbers.
- Record facts separately from opinions in the notes template below.
- Get consent before recording, and before collecting any sample data.

## Opening (2 min)
- Thank them; explain it's research, about 40 minutes, no sales pitch.
- Confirm consent to take notes or record.
- Their role, business size, what they sell, roughly how many credit customers.

## 1. Invoicing (5 min)
1. Walk me through how an invoice gets created after an order ships.
2. Which system produces invoices? Who issues them?
3. Roughly how many invoices a month?
4. Do you invoice in more than one currency? *(A-08)*

## 2. Credit terms (4 min)
5. How do you decide what terms a customer gets?
6. When did you last change a customer's terms? What prompted it?

## 3. Monitoring receivables (6 min)
7. How do you know today what you're owed? Show me if possible.
8. How often do you look? Who looks? *(A-03)*
9. When did a receivables figure last turn out to be wrong? What caused it?

## 4. Chasing overdue invoices (5 min)
10. Tell me about the last overdue invoice you chased. How did you decide it was the one to chase?
11. What tools or reminders do you use?
12. How much time a week goes on chasing?

## 5. Receiving and reconciling payments (8 min)
13. How do payments arrive (EFT, card, cheque, BPAY)?
14. Walk me through matching last week's receipts to invoices.
15. What happens when a customer pays several invoices in one transfer, or pays part of one?
16. How long does reconciliation take each week or month?
17. What do you do with money you can't match?

## 6. Payment history and data (4 min)
18. Do you track how each customer usually pays? Where?
19. If you exported your invoices and payments today, what format would they be in? Do customers have a code in your system? *(A-02, A-07)*
20. Would you re-export everything each time, or only changes? *(A-09)*

## 7. Decisions and gaps (4 min)
21. What financial decision have you made recently where you lacked information?
22. What do you currently pay for accounting or finance tools?

## 8. Value (optional, last) (3 min)
23. If something reliably showed what you're owed, by whom and how late they usually pay, what would that change for you?
24. What would stop you from using it? *(A-04)*
25. Would you share an anonymised export so we can test with real-shaped data? (See `data-requirements.md`.)
